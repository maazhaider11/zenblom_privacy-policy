# Privacy Policy for Zen Bloom

**Last updated: July 14, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Zen Bloom** (the "Game"), published by
**Techzam Solutions** ("we", "us"), handles information when you play. Zen Bloom
is a single-player puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account and we do
not knowingly collect information that directly identifies you (such as your
name or email). However, the Game **displays third-party advertisements**, and
those ad services collect certain data as described below.

By playing Zen Bloom you agree to this Policy.

## Summary

- We do **not** collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, or
  precise GPS location.
- The Game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Advertising services may collect
  device and usage data (including your device's advertising ID) to show and
  measure ads.
- Your game progress and settings are stored **locally on your device**.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Information collected

### 1. Information you provide
Zen Bloom has no sign-up, login, or profile. We do not request or store your
name, email address, phone number, or similar personal details.

### 2. Data stored locally on your device
To let you keep playing, the Game saves gameplay data on your device: your
current level, coins, gems, hearts, purchased in-game boosts, and sound
settings. This data stays on your device, is used only by the Game, and is
removed if you uninstall the Game.

### 3. Data collected automatically by advertising & related services
The Game uses **Google AdMob** (and Google's advertising partners) to serve
ads. To deliver, cap, and measure ads and to help prevent fraud, these services
may automatically collect and process:

- Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
- Device information (model, operating system version, language, device
  settings)
- IP address and coarse/approximate location derived from it
- App interaction and ad-interaction data (e.g., ads viewed or clicked, and
  general app usage related to ads)
- Diagnostic/crash information

We do **not** control what data Google and its partners collect through their
SDKs. Their handling of this data is governed by their own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob & advertising: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## How we use information

- To display and support advertising in the Game.
- To save and restore your game progress and preferences on your device.
- To keep the Game working, diagnose problems, and improve stability.

## Advertising and your choices

Ads may be **personalized** or **non-personalized** depending on your settings
and region. You can control ad personalization:

- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads — where you can **reset** or **delete**
  your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.
- **Europe/UK (GDPR):** where required, the Game will ask for your consent
  before serving personalized ads via a consent prompt; you may choose
  non-personalized ads.
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** we do not "sell" personal information for money.
  Sharing of identifiers with ad partners for advertising can be limited using
  the Android advertising controls above.

## Sharing of information

We share data only with the advertising and infrastructure providers needed to
run the Game (primarily **Google**). We do not sell your personal information
and do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

## Children's privacy

Zen Bloom is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under the age required by your local law
(e.g., 13 in the U.S.). Where the Game is made available to children, ads are
requested using child-appropriate, families-policy-compliant settings. If you
believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take
appropriate steps.

## Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the
Game. Data collected by advertising providers is retained according to their
own policies.

## Security

We rely on the security practices of the platforms and providers we use (Google
Play and Google advertising services). No method of transmission or storage is
100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this
page's location with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued play after
changes means you accept the revised Policy.
