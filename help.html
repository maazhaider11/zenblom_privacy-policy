How to play

Zen Bloom is a relaxing tile-match puzzle. Each level is a picture built from stacked nature tiles — leaves, blossoms, fruit, sun and water.

  1. Tap a free tile — any tile that isn't covered and has an open left or right edge — to lift it up into your tray at the top.
  2. Match a pair. When two tiles of the same kind meet in the tray, they clear automatically and you score points.
  3. Clear the whole board before the timer runs out to win the level.
  4. Mind the tray. It holds only 4 tiles. If it fills up with tiles that can't be matched, you lose the level — so plan which tiles to lift.
Tip: tiles glow when they're free to tap. If you're stuck, use a Hint to highlight a helpful move (3 per level).

Coins, gems & hearts

  • Coins — earned every time you clear a level. Spend them on cheaper items in the Shop.
  • Gems — rarer; earned for 2- and 3-star clears. Used for premium items like extra timer boosts and the Golden Tiles theme.
  • Hearts — used to retry a level you didn't finish. You earn a heart for a perfect 3-star clear, and can buy more in the Shop.

There are no real-money purchases. Every coin, gem and heart is earned by playing.

Boosts & the Shop

  • +2 Hints (for 5 levels) — more hints while you learn a tricky stretch.
  • +30s Timer (for 2 levels) — a little extra breathing room.
  • Golden Tiles — a permanent shiny tile theme.
  • Hearts — buy packs with coins or gems.

Frequently asked questions

How do I earn more gems?

Finish levels with 2 or 3 stars. Stars depend on how few hints you use and how much time is left when you clear the board.

The tray filled up and I lost — why?

The tray holds 4 tiles at once. Only lift a tile when you can pair it soon, or when lifting it frees the tiles you need. Matching pairs leave the tray instantly.

Does the game use the internet or ads?

Your progress is saved on your device. The game may show ads from Google AdMob; see our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I turn off music, sound or notifications?

Open Settings from the gear icon and toggle Music, SFX or Notifications.

I got a new phone — can I move my progress?

Progress is stored locally on each device, so it doesn't transfer automatically when you switch phones.
